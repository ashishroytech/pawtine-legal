Common questions

A reminder didn't fire. Check Settings > Notifications > View scheduled reminders to confirm it's actually scheduled, and Settings > Notifications > Open Notification Settings to confirm notifications are allowed for Pawtine in iOS.

I want to cancel or change my subscription. Manage it directly in your iPhone's Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions, or from Settings > Subscription > Manage Subscription inside the app.

I want to delete my data or account. Settings > Your data has options to delete your local data, your cloud backup, or your account entirely.
